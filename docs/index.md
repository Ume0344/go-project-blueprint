# Two Siblings - Mars and Earth

A long time ago, Earth and Mars were like two siblings who both had water. Earth held on tight, 
building oceans, rivers, and even turning that water into life — fish, trees, humans, and yes, coffee. 
Mars, on the other hand, was careless. It lost its atmosphere, and with it, most of its water just packed up and left.

Now Earth is the blue, lively sibling, while Mars is the dry, dusty one looking a bit jealous. 
We keep sending robots there, asking, “Hey, did you really once have oceans?” Mars just shrugs 
and says, “Yeah, but I let them slip away.” A funny reminder for us: don’t take our water for granted!
